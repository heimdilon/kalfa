One-line: the 44px header bar above a column, panel or dialog.

```jsx
<PanelHeader icon="messages-square" title="auth akışı incelemesi" subtitle="glm-5.3 · 14:29"
  actions={<IconButton icon="more-horizontal" label="Daha fazla"/>} />
```

Title in sans semibold, meta in mono muted, actions right. Height is always `--titlebar-height` (44px).
